── Jab koi threshold badalne ko kahe ────────────────────────────────────────
Before changing that, run prototype/reachability.py. Two parameters that each
look fine can combine to make a gate unreachable at every volatility. That
already happened once with b_entry_max_extreme_atr_mult — 1.2 plus a 0.45
buffer against a 1.40 ceiling could never pass, and reading it did not catch it.

── Jab detection zero ho ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Detection is zero. Do not build further. Use the funnel in prototype/score.py
and tell me which stage is losing it: no level at origin, level but not Grade A,
Grade A but no ALERT, or ALERT but no setup. Those are four different problems.

── Jab naya rule ya setup add karna chahe ───────────────────────────────────
Read specs/11-LEARNING-LOOP.md §2 and §3 first. Three questions: what is the
mechanism — why does the market do this, not what does the data show? What does
it cost in precision and in levels per day? And is a threshold change enough
instead? If the honest answer is that no rule change is justified, say that.

── Candle dialogue ke dauraan (spec 13) — har session me dohrao ─────────────
This is a dialogue to FIND candidates, not to accept them. Do not change
params.yaml during this conversation, however obvious it looks. Write candidates
to annotations/candidates/ and we run them through the spec 12 pipeline
afterwards — measured, cost-checked, regression-tested. Maximum 3 candidates
this session. And if the honest answer is "there was nothing here, you just
remember the move," say that instead of finding me a rule.

── Jab annotation process kare (spec 12) ────────────────────────────────────
Check that this session's annotation has entries in all three buckets. If
false_positive is empty, tell me — an annotation with only missed levels is not
calibration, it is inflation, and it will push the engine toward more lines every
single session. Also cross-check my stated reasons against the measured numbers.
If I say four rejections and the data says two, tell me. Do not quietly encode
my mistake.

── Jab koi report sundar lage ───────────────────────────────────────────────
Is this net of costs? Is costs.yaml verified and how old is last_verified? What
does r_realised_vs_planned say? A good-looking first result is a reason to
suspect a look-ahead leak and re-run the truncation test, not to celebrate.

── Jab kahe phase poora ho gaya ─────────────────────────────────────────────
Show me the phase gate output from BUILD-BRIEF.md §2 and the detection counts
from §4b. A phase with skipped or xfail tests is not done, and in this build the
last ten percent is usually the part that handles the case that loses money.

── Jab main koi rule add karne ko kahu jo galat lage ────────────────────────
If I ask for something that contradicts CLAUDE.md §6, or that would need a gate
loosened to make more trades happen, push back and show me the number. I would
rather be argued with now than find out in P9.
