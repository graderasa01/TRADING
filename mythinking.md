# 1. Mera setup — 4 TF, par har ek ko ALAG WAQT par dekhta hu

| TF | Kab dekhta hu | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Sirf pre-market, 2 minute. **Din me dobara nahi.** | PDH, PDL, PDC — 3 number. Bas. |
| **15-min** | Sirf jab 15m candle band ho (har 15 min) | **Levels + bias.** Map. |
| **5-min** | Ye mera **ghar** hai. Yahan sochta hu. | **Structure** — control kiske paas hai |
| **1-min** | **Sirf jab price level par ho.** Warna band. | **Trigger + SL.** Bas. |

Aur ek asli baat — **1m chart din bhar khula rehna sabse bada leak hai.** Wo tumhe har 4 minute me ek "setup" dikhayega. Main usko band rakhta hu aur sirf level ke paas kholta hu.

# 4. Live market me main teen MODE me hota hu — aur bas ek me

Ye poore jawab ka dil hai. Har waqt main **exactly ek** mode me hu:

## Mode 1 — WATCH (din ka 70%)

Price khaali jagah me hai. **Zero order. 1m chart band.** Main har 5m candle par board update karta hu — 10 second — aur baaki waqt kuch aur kar raha hota hu.

**Yahi wo mode hai jo log hold nahi kar paate.** Aur yahi wajah hai ki wo haarte hain. Khaali baithna kaam hai, kaam ki kami nahi.

## Mode 2 — ALERT (din ka 25%)

Price kisi marked level se **20 point ke andar** aa gayi.

Ab: 1m chart khulta hai. IF-THEN likhta hu. **SL ka number aur size candle banne se PEHLE calculate.** Haath keyboard par. **Par order abhi bhi nahi.**

## Mode 3 — IN (din ka 5%)

Position khuli hai. Ab mera kaam **poori tarah badal jaata hai** — main analysis band kar deta hu. Ab sirf ek cheez dekhta hu: **thesis toota ya nahi.**

**Sabse badi galti:** log din bhar Mode 2 me rehte hain (1m ghoorte hue), aur wo dhire-dhire **galti se** Mode 3 ban jaata hai. Trade decision nahi hota, **haadsa** hota hai.

# 5. Har candle par main SIRF ye 5 dekhta hu — 10 second

5m candle band hoti hai. Bas itna:

1. **Close level ke kis taraf?** (sirf body)
2. **Close apni range me kahan?** (upar 1/3, beech, neeche 1/3)
3. **Size — pichli 5 se badi ya chhoti?**
4. **Board badla?** (naya high/low, swing toota, level flip)
5. **Agli candle ka IF-THEN — ek line**

Bas. Agar isme 15 second se zyada lag raha hai, matlab main over-think kar raha hu.

**Aur main kya NAHI karta:** pattern nahi dhundhta, nayi line nahi kheenchta, doosre stocks nahi dekhta, news nahi padhta, kisi ki tweet nahi dekhta.

# 7. Entry — main sirf 3 SETUP lunga. Teen. Bas.

Zyada setup = zyada nuksan, zyada munafa nahi. Har extra setup tumhe **wait karna bhula deta hai.**

## Setup A — FLIP RETEST *(roz ki roti, sabse zyada hit rate)*

Level body-close se toota → price wapas level par aayi → level ne **hold** kiya (1m par higher low) → entry.

- **Entry:** level ke 3-5 pts upar (body edge par, wick tip par nahi)
- **SL:** us 1m higher-low ke 8-10 pts neeche
- **Kyun best:** SL sabse chhota, level bilkul clear, aur trend ke **saath** hai

## Setup B — SWEEP RECLAIM *(best R:R, kam milta hai)*

Level par wick nikli → body wapas andar close → agli candle confirm.

- **Entry:** reclaim candle ke close par
- **SL:** sweep wick ke tip se 8-10 pts paar
- **Kyun:** stops pehle hi saaf ho gaye, raasta khaali hai

## Setup C — RANGE BREAK RETEST *(sirf LAMBI range ke baad)*

Range **20+ candle** ki thi, tight hoti gayi, body close se nikli → phir break point par **wapas** aayi → entry.

- **Kabhi nahi:** breakout candle par hi entry. **Hamesha** retest.

## Main entry KABHI nahi lunga:

- Beech khaali jagah me — candle kitni bhi sundar ho
- Breakout candle par turant
- **11:15–13:30** me
- **3rd touch** par reversal
- Jab SL **35 pts se bada** ho
- Jab aaj **3 trade** ho gaye
- Jab **pichli 2 lag gayi**
- Jab main "chhoot na jaye" mehsoos kar raha hu

Aakhri point technical nahi hai, par **sabse zyada paisa wahi bachata hai.**

# 8. Stop Loss — yahan main sabse sakht hu

## Kahan

**Level par nahi.** Wick tip ke paar. Kyunki level hi wo jagah hai jahan **sabke stop** hain — aur sweep wahin tak jaata hai.

```
SL = wick tip ± MAX(10 pts, 0.25 × 1m ATR14)
```

Fixed 10 nahi — kyunki 1m ATR kabhi 15 hai kabhi 70. Fixed number shaant din par theek hai, tez din par tumhe har baar ud'ta hai.

## Kitna

| R | Faisla |
|---|---|
| **< 12 pts** | Bahut tight. Shor hi maar dega. **Skip ya ruko.** |
| **15–25 pts** | Sweet spot |
| **> 35 pts** | R:R marr gaya. **Skip.** |

## Size SL se aata hai — SL size se nahi. *(ye sabse important line hai)*

```
Lots = aaj ka risk limit ÷ R
```

R 30 pts hai to **aadhi size.** Aur SL ko **kabhi** size fit karne ke liye chhota nahi karna. Ye ulta karne se hi log ek trade me mahine ki kamai de dete hain.

## Do exit — aur DOOSRA zyada important hai

**1. Hard SL** — system me lagi hui, hamesha. **Mental stop, stop nahi hota.** Wo ek ummeed hoti hai.

**2. Thesis SL (invalidation)** — koi candle body wapas galat taraf close kare → **turant market exit**, chahe hard SL 15 pts door ho.

Ye doosra rule kisi bhi entry rule se zyada paisa bachata hai. Kyunki asli nuksan chhote loss se nahi hota — **us loss se hota hai jise tumne pura SL tak khinchne diya jabki tumhe minute 2 par pata tha ki galat ho.**

## Kabhi nahi

SL badhana. Average down. SL "ek minute ke liye" hatana. Wahi fail setup teesri baar lena.

**T1 hit hone par:** SL breakeven par — **compulsory**, "achha lag raha hai" par nahi.

# 9. Scalping — ab poori sachai, kyunki yahan sabse zyada paisa marta hai

Maths dekho, feeling nahi:

Bank Nifty options me entry + exit ka slippage + brokerage ≈ **3–6 point round trip.**

| Target | Cost ka hissa |
|---|---|
| 15 pts | **20–40% gross ka** |
| 40 pts | ~10% |

Matlab: **scalping is system ko chalane ka sabse MUSHKIL tarika hai, sabse aasaan nahi.**

Wo *safe* lagta hai — chhota SL, jaldi bahar. Par cost ka bojh kroor hai, aur **frequency har galti ko multiply karti hai.** 10 trade me tumhari discipline 1 trade jaisi nahi rehti.

**Agar main scalp karta, to sirf ye:**

- Sirf **Setup A aur B.** Breakout scalp kabhi nahi.
- Sirf do windows: **9:30–11:15, 13:30–14:45**
- **Cost ke BAAD** minimum 1:2 — R 15 hai to target **35+**, 30 nahi
- **Max 3 trade** — scalping ka asli katil over-trading hai, galat entry nahi
- **ATM option ya future** — far OTM me slippage hi tumhara pura edge kha jaata hai

Aur meri imaandaar salah: **kam aur badi trade, zyada aur chhoti se behtar hai.** Isliye nahi ki setup behtar hai — isliye ki **cost per trade fixed hai, aur galtiyan frequency ke saath badhti hain.**

# 10. Position khuli hai — ab mera kaam BILKUL ALAG hai

Mode 3 me main analyst nahi hu, **manager** hu.

- **Analysis band.** Naye level nahi, naya view nahi, doosri taraf ki soch nahi.
- Har candle par **sirf ek sawaal:** thesis toota?
- **T1:** 1.5R ya nearest obstacle — jo pehle aaye. **50% book. SL breakeven.**
- **Runner:** har naye higher-low ke neeche trail (5m par, 1m par nahi — 1m par trail karke tum har winner jaldi kaat doge)
- **TIME STOP:** 5+ candle ho gayi aur trade aage nahi badhi → **nikal jao**, jo bhi bhaav hai.

Time stop bahut kam log use karte hain. **Jo trade kaam nahi kar rahi, wo pehle se ek losing trade hai** — bas abhi P&L me nahi dikhi.

# 12. Market ke baad — 10 minute, ye maaf nahi

Yahan sudhar hota hai, aur **yahi sab chhodte hain.**

Har trade ka log:

| Column | |
|---|---|
| Setup | A / B / C |
| **Sab rule follow kiye? Y/N** | ← **sabse important column** |
| R result | +1.8 / −1 |
| Screenshot | entry ke waqt ka |

Aur hafte me ek baar **sirf ye** dekho:

> **"Rule-follow karke haare" losses ko "rule-tod kar haare" losses se alag karo.**

- Rule follow karke haare = **business ka kharcha.** Normal.
- Rule tod kar haare = **asli samasya.**

Agar tum paisa haar rahe ho aur 70% loss rule-tod wale hain — **tumhara system theek hai, discipline nahi.** Ye bilkul alag ilaaj hai. Log system badalte rehte hain jabki system galti nahi thi.
